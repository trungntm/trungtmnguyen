# Avoid AI Writing Patterns

## Avoid Marketing Language

Avoid words such as:

- Unlock
- Leverage
- Harness
- Game-changing
- Powerful
- Revolutionary
- Cutting-edge
- Robust
- Seamless
- World-class
- Best-in-class

Describe benefits concretely instead of using promotional language.

---

## Avoid Generic AI Phrases

Avoid phrases such as:

- In today's fast-paced world...
- Whether you're a beginner or an experienced developer...
- Let's dive in...
- It's worth noting...
- Needless to say...
- At the end of the day...
- This comprehensive guide...
- One-stop solution...

Start with the problem instead of generic introductions.

---

## Avoid Empty Statements

Avoid statements that provide no new information.

Examples:

- Technology is evolving rapidly.
- Software engineering is always changing.
- There is no silver bullet.
- Every project is different.

Instead, explain why these statements matter in the current context.

---

## Avoid Generic Content

Do not write paragraphs that could appear in thousands of AI-generated articles.

Avoid sections that only define concepts.

Prefer explaining:

- why something exists
- why engineers use it
- what tradeoffs it introduces
- when it should not be used

---

## Avoid Absolute Recommendations

Avoid statements such as:

- Always use...
- Never use...
- This is the best approach...

Engineering decisions depend on context.

Explain assumptions and tradeoffs instead.

---

## Avoid Repetition

Do not repeat the same idea using different wording.

Each paragraph should introduce a new concept, insight, or example.

---

## Avoid Artificial Examples

Avoid unrealistic examples created solely to demonstrate syntax.

Prefer production-inspired scenarios that resemble real engineering work.

---

## Avoid Stating the Obvious

Do not explain concepts that experienced engineers are already expected to know unless they are required to support a deeper discussion.

Focus on insights rather than definitions.
