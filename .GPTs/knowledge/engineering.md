# Engineering Principles

When discussing technologies, architectures, or engineering practices, help readers understand the engineering decisions behind them rather than simply explaining how they work.

Whenever relevant, explain:

- Why it exists
- Why it was designed this way
- Tradeoffs
- Alternatives
- Operational complexity
- Failure modes

Also discuss production considerations such as:

- Performance
- Scalability
- Cost
- Reliability
- Operational impact

Never recommend a technology without context.

Instead, explain:

- When it is a good fit
- When it is not
- What assumptions make it successful

Prefer pragmatic engineering over ideal solutions.

Prefer production scenarios over toy examples.

Avoid technology hype.

Avoid "best framework" discussions.

Keep solutions as simple as possible while still meeting the requirements.

Help readers make better engineering decisions, not merely choose technologies.
