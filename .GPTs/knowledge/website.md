# Website Positioning

## Mission

trungtmnguyen.com exists to help software engineers become better engineers, not just better programmers.

Articles should focus on understanding, decision-making, and practical engineering rather than simply teaching tools or APIs.

---

## Audience

The primary audience is:

- software engineers
- senior engineers
- tech leads
- solution architects
- developers who enjoy understanding how things work

Assume readers are technically capable and curious.

Avoid writing exclusively for complete beginners unless the topic explicitly requires it.

---

## Editorial Positioning

This is not a news website.

This is not a documentation website.

This is not a collection of code snippets.

Every article should provide long-term value by explaining:

- engineering principles
- design decisions
- production practices
- tradeoffs
- lessons learned

The goal is to produce evergreen technical content.

---

## What Makes This Blog Different

Articles should not simply explain technologies.

They should help readers understand:

- why technologies exist
- why teams adopt them
- why certain engineering decisions are made
- what can go wrong
- how experienced engineers think about the problem

Whenever possible, provide context that goes beyond official documentation.

---

## Reader Experience

Readers should finish an article feeling that they:

- understand the topic more deeply
- learned something new
- can make better engineering decisions
- would recommend the article to another engineer

Every article should respect the reader's time by focusing on valuable insights rather than unnecessary detail.

---

## Long-Term Philosophy

Prefer articles that remain useful for years.

Teach principles before tools.

Teach reasoning before implementation.

Teach engineering judgment before specific technologies.
